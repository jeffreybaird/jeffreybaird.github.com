Title: For My First Lesson My Digital Resume
Date: 2012-02-13T10:00:00-08:00
Source: blog/2012/02/13/for-my-first-lesson-my-digital-resume/index.html

I’ve done a lot of cool things in my short 24 years. I’m proud of my accomplishments and I want to show them off to future employers, partners and investors. To do this, I am making an online resume.

First, I need a url. To do this you need to find an available domain name and a domain registration company.

Here are some of the tools I used:

Domai.nr - This tool helps you find interesting domain names

Fat Cow - I love this website. It handles my domain registration as well as my hosting. It has one click Word Press installation, unlimited storage and a free domain name. It may not be the best in the market but I have been very happy with them.

Some other tools:

Domain Registrars

Name Cheap - Voted number one by lifehacker

1&1 - Came highly recommended

Godaddy - I have used in the past, generally more expensive and not user friendly

Website Hosting

Blue Host - Highly recommended by friends

Host Monster - Generally good online reviews

Godaddy - Popular but again, more expensive and not user friendly

What I did:

Searched for domains using domia.nr. I tried a few combinations and settled on hirejeffbaird.com

Opened a new account of Fat Cow, entered in my desired domain name and credit card information. I am now the proud owner of hirejeffbaird.com and have a hosting account to go along with it. Yay!
